# Known limitations

## Cloudflare

- **Workers on `workers.dev` cannot call each other** (error 1042). Put at
  least the API server on a custom domain ([Deploy](/self-hosting/deploy#5-domains)).
- **A D1 database lives in one region**, and has Cloudflare's size limits per
  database. Check Cloudflare's current D1 limits for your plan.

## Carriers

Every carrier integration is tested against the carriers' recorded answers,
and the handoff, sync and webhook paths run end to end on a real database.

- **EcoTrack couriers**: verified live on an EcoTrack test account — home and
  stop-desk parcels, bulk creation, edits, notes, labels, validation,
  cancelling, history and status sync. Not yet seen live: a parcel delivered,
  refused or returned, so returns are verified only against EcoTrack's
  documented API.
- **The 2019 wilayas (49–58)**: Wocart's commune list still files their
  communes under their former wilaya, and each new wilaya holds only its
  capital. Carriers on the 58-wilaya split (EcoTrack, Yalidine) refuse a
  parcel to such a commune — about 60 of them. About 110 more communes are
  spelled too differently from EcoTrack's list to be matched; **Sync delivery
  zones** on the company lists them.
- **ZR Express** lets a company rename its states; a name ZR's standard
  workflow does not know is recorded as an unknown status until the merchant
  maps it in the company's settings.
- **NOEST, Guepex and the EcoTrack couriers** send no webhooks. They are
  checked every five minutes: up to 20 open parcels per company per run, each
  at most every 30 minutes, and at once with **Sync**.
- **Carrier payouts** (a carrier paying the merchant the cash it collected)
  are kept but not reconciled.

## Orders and shipments

- An order has at most one shipment. Shipping it again means voiding the
  first, which is possible only before it is out for delivery or after the
  carrier cancelled it.
- Handing off in bulk takes up to 100 orders at a time.

## Meta, webhooks and Telegram

These deliver through the same tested event engine, but have not yet run
against real Meta pixels, merchants' own systems or merchants' bots in
production.

- **Meta**: an order from a store with no domain set is sent without its page
  address (`event_source_url`).
- **Webhooks**: an endpoint is refused when it is not https, carries
  credentials or names a private address — judged by its name, not by what
  it resolves to. Attempts run one after another, each up to 15 seconds, so
  many slow endpoints delay the others until the next run.
- **Telegram**: one private chat per store; groups and channels are not
  supported. Telegram allows about one message a second to a chat, so a burst
  (a carrier sync delivering twenty parcels) may be retried a few minutes
  later. Changing bots leaves the old bot's webhook set; its updates are
  refused.
