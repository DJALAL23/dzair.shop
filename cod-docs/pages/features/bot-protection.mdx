# Bot protection

With bot protection on, the order form is guarded by
[Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/): a
check, usually invisible, that tells shoppers from bots. Turnstile is free.

It is off by default, works alongside [phone verification](/features/phone-verification),
and never applies to orders your team creates in the dashboard.

## Before you start

In the Cloudflare dashboard, open **Turnstile → Add widget**:

- choose **Managed** mode;
- list every domain your storefront is served on, including its custom
  domain. On a domain that is not listed, the check fails.

The widget has a **site key**, public, and a **secret key**, which only the
API server uses.

## Turn it on

Dashboard → **Apps → Turnstile**: paste both keys, turn on **Protect the
order form**, and save. Once saved, only the secret's last 4 characters are
shown; saving with a field empty keeps the stored key.

Through the API: `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/apps/turnstile`. Turning it off
keeps the keys; removing the App forgets them.

## What the shopper sees

Usually nothing: the check completes on its own. A visitor Cloudflare finds
risky is shown a checkbox. A check expires after 5 minutes and is used once;
the form takes a fresh one on the next submit.

## Orders are never lost to it

- **If Cloudflare's check cannot be reached**, the order is placed anyway.
- **A missing, invalid, expired or reused check is refused**, and the shopper
  submits again.

## Testing

Cloudflare's test keys work on any domain, `localhost` included:

| Site key | Secret key | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1x00000000000000000000AA` | `1x0000000000000000000000000000000AA` | Always passes |
| `2x00000000000000000000AB` | `2x0000000000000000000000000000000AA` | Always fails |

## Permissions

`turnstile:read` to see the App, `turnstile:manage` to change, turn off or
remove it.
