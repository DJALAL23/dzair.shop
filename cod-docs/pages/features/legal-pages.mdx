# Legal pages

Meta checks that a store has clear policy pages before it runs the store's
ads. Every Wocart store therefore starts with four pages, written for a
cash-on-delivery store selling in Algeria:

- Terms and Conditions
- Privacy Policy
- Refund and Return Policy
- Shipping Policy

They are published at `/pages/<slug>`, linked from the footer, and linked
from the order form by a consent line, from the moment the store exists, in
Arabic, French and English.

**They are a starting point, not legal advice.** They describe how
cash-on-delivery selling works (no online payment, confirmation by phone,
inspection at the door, home or stop-desk delivery) with reference to
Algeria's e-commerce (18-05), consumer protection (09-03) and data protection
(18-07) laws. Review them for your business.

## Edit a page

Dashboard → **Pages**: pick a page, edit its title and content in the
rich-text editor, optionally its SEO title and description, and save. A page
still in its original wording is marked **Needs review**; saving marks it
**Reviewed**. The mark is for you: it never hides a page.

Each language has its own tab. **Reset to CodFlow template** on one of the
four puts back the original wording for that language, after a confirmation.
(The dashboard still uses the name CodFlow here.)

## Business details

Dashboard → **Pages → Business details** holds the facts the pages are written
from: the legal name, the commercial register (RC), the tax ID (NIF), the
address, the contact email and phone, the return window in days (0 means no
returns after delivery; refusing at the door is always free), and the
delivery-time range.

A field left blank is left out of the pages: they never show a placeholder.
Saving the details does not rewrite a page you have edited; it changes what a
later **Reset to CodFlow template** produces.

## Pages of your own

**New page** creates a page you write yourself: an FAQ, an About page. It
starts as a draft; publish it, and choose whether it appears in the footer.
Unlike the four legal pages, it can be deleted.

## Where pages appear

- **At `/pages/<slug>`.** The slug can change; a link already shared with the
  old one stops working, and the editor warns you before you save.
- **In the footer**: every published page shown in the footer, in the order
  you set.
- **On the order form**: "By placing this order, you agree to our Terms and
  Refund Policy", linked to both, while both are published.

## A store without them

A store created before legal pages existed can add them from **Pages → Add
terms, privacy, refund & shipping**; it creates only the missing ones. Through
the API: `POST /api/store-pages/seed-defaults`.

## Permissions

`store_pages:read` to see the pages, `store_pages:manage` to edit them.

## For developers

The pages are under `/api/store-pages`, the business details under
`/api/store-pages/legal-profile`, and storefronts read a published page with
the [Storefront SDK](/sdk/storefront/content).
